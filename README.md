## About me

I am an MSc Business Analytics graduate from Aston University (2025–2026), with a BBA from India (CGPA 8.37/10). My dissertation, *Business Analytics and Decision-Making: A Study of How UK Businesses Use Data in Strategic Planning*, studied 248 UK SMEs and compared regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting and XGBoost models for predicting SME growth.

I previously worked as a **Project Associate at Centific, Hyderabad, India (July – November 2025)**, on data-focused projects supporting AI and analytics workflows — including data quality analysis across large-scale datasets, structured data processing and validation pipelines, and project delivery with cross-functional global teams.

My core competencies are **Business Analysis, Strategic Decision Making, and KPI Monitoring & Performance Analysis**. During my undergraduate studies I was a member of the Strategic Decision Making Club.
